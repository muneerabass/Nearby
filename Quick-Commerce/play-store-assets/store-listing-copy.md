## Full description

Nearby brings your neighborhood essentials to your door. Browse products from nearby stores, build your cart, and place convenient cash-on-delivery orders in a few taps.

Shop everyday groceries and essentials from local stores. Set your delivery location, explore categories, search for products, adjust quantities, and review your order before checkout.

Nearby helps you:

• Discover products from stores near you
• Browse by category or search by name
• Add items to your cart and update quantities easily
• Save delivery addresses for faster checkout
• Track order progress from placement to delivery
• Reorder items you buy regularly
• Receive a simple, reliable cash-on-delivery experience

Whether you need a few daily essentials or a larger household order, Nearby makes local shopping simple and convenient.

Availability, delivery coverage, product selection, and delivery times may vary by location.
